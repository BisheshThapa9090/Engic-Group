Project Overview

Nestled in the serene landscapes of Tanahun district, this stunning eco-friendly modern home represents the perfect blend of sustainable architecture and contemporary living. Designed and executed by Engic Group, this 4BHK residence was created for a family seeking harmony with nature while enjoying modern comforts and amenities.

Built with passive solar heating principles and utilizing local materials, this home minimizes environmental impact while maximizing energy efficiency. The design incorporates large windows for natural light, thermal mass walls for temperature regulation, and locally sourced stone and timber that complement the surrounding landscape.

The home features spacious living areas, a modern kitchen with energy-efficient appliances, natural stone flooring, and beautiful outdoor spaces that connect residents with the stunning Tanahun countryside.

Key Features & Amenities

  • Seismic-resistant RCC frame structure (Zone V compliant)
  • Passive solar heating system for energy efficiency
  • Natural stone flooring and locally sourced timber
  • Energy-efficient modular kitchen with eco-friendly fittings
  • Double-glazed windows for optimal insulation
  • Large terrace with panoramic mountain views
  • Native landscaping with drought-resistant plants
  • Solar water heating system with backup
  • Custom-designed wooden wardrobes and furniture
