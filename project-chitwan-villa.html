Project Overview

Chitwan Villa represents the pinnacle of modern residential design in Chitwan. This luxury 3BHK home was designed and executed by Engic Group for a client who desired a perfect blend of aesthetics, functionality, and structural resilience.

Built with earthquake-resistant technology and featuring smart home integration, this villa sets a new standard for luxury living in the Chitwan district. Every aspect, from the foundation to the finishing touches, was meticulously planned and executed under the supervision of our senior structural engineers.

Key Features & Amenities

  • Seismic-resistant RCC frame structure
  • Smart home automation (lighting, security, temperature)
  • Premium Italian marble flooring
  • Modular kitchen with imported fittings
  • Energy-efficient double-glazed windows
  • Rooftop terrace with panoramic views
  • Landscaped garden with irrigation system
  • Solar water heating system
