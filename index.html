Engineering Services Built With Precision

At Engic Group, we combine innovative engineering with architectural excellence to deliver solutions that stand the test of time. Our comprehensive services are designed to transform your vision into reality with unmatched quality and attention to detail.

Architectural & Structural Design

Complete analysis, design, and planning for seismic-resistant structures with full building code compliance and modern architectural planning.

Exterior 3D Visualization

Photorealistic exterior renders, virtual property walkthroughs, and detailed material and lighting studies to bring your project to life before construction.

Interior Design & Construction

Complete 2D/3D interior layout design, detailed construction drawings, and material selection optimized for aesthetics and budget.

Exterior & Interior Renovation

Structural assessment and retrofitting, modernization planning, and material upgrades to give existing structures a new lease on life.

Estimation & Property Valuation

Detailed cost analysis, accurate material and labor estimation, and comprehensive budget planning backed by real market data.

Construction & Site Supervision

Daily on-site supervision, rigorous quality control and assurance, and thorough progress documentation for flawless project execution.

Environmental Impact Assessment

Environmental baseline studies, impact forecasting and analysis, and mitigation strategy planning to ensure sustainable development.

Initial Environmental Examination

Site evaluation and assessment, environmental risk screening, and full IEE documentation preparation for government approval.

Project Consulting & Advisory

Project feasibility studies, risk assessment and management, and technical advisory services for successful project completion.
