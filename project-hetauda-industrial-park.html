Project Overview

Hetauda Industrial Park is a landmark industrial development designed to meet the growing demand for modern manufacturing and warehousing facilities in Nepal. Located strategically in Hetauda, this industrial complex offers world-class infrastructure for businesses looking to establish or expand their operations.

Engic Group was responsible for the master planning, design, and construction of this state-of-the-art industrial park. The complex features 12 manufacturing units, warehouse spaces, and administrative buildings, all designed to international standards for efficiency, safety, and sustainability.

With robust infrastructure, including dedicated power supply, water treatment facilities, and modern logistics infrastructure, the Hetauda Industrial Park is positioned to become a hub for industrial growth in the region.

Key Features & Amenities

  • 12 modern manufacturing units
  • Large warehouse spaces with high ceilings
  • Dedicated power supply with backup
  • Water treatment and recycling facility
  • Wide internal roads and logistics infrastructure
  • Security systems with 24/7 surveillance
  • Landscaped green areas and employee amenities
  • Sustainable design with energy efficiency
  • Administrative buildings with modern offices
  • Fire safety systems and emergency response
