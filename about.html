Our Story

Engineering Excellence in the Heart of Nepal

Engic Group was founded with a simple yet powerful vision: to transform Nepal's construction landscape through innovative engineering, sustainable design, and unwavering commitment to quality. What started as a small team of passionate engineers has grown into one of Nepal's most trusted civil engineering and architectural firms.

Our journey has been defined by a relentless pursuit of excellence. From the bustling streets of Kathmandu to the remote corners of the country, our projects stand as testaments to our dedication to quality, safety, and sustainability.

Today, Engic Group is proud to be at the forefront of Nepal's construction industry, delivering projects that not only meet but exceed the expectations of our clients and communities.
